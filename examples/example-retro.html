RETROSPECTIVE

Launch week — auth service public beta

The identity squad took the auth service to public beta and ran the first real week of external traffic. This retrospective captures what worked, what did not, and the actions we are taking into the GA push.

Completed 2026-08-11 Facilitator: M. Chen Duration: 1 week

Metrics

4.2k signups in the first week
99.9% uptime during launch
3 Sev-2 incidents

Timeline

  • Aug 03
    Beta announcement. The public beta went live with a canary cohort of invited teams, and the status page flipped to "operational".
  • Aug 04
    Traffic spike. Signups jumped past the forecast as the announcement circulated, and the canary cohort absorbed the first real load surge.
  • Aug 05
    Token-rotation bug found. Concurrent tab bursts surfaced a refresh-token rotation race under a single token family.
  • Aug 06
    Hotfix shipped. A targeted hotfix reduced the race window while the idempotent rotation work was planned.
  • Aug 07
    First retention read. The earliest cohort showed day-1 and day-3 retention above the internal targets.
  • Aug 11
    Retrospective. Reviewed the three incidents and agreed the action items below.

What went well

  • The canary cohort caught the worst of the load issues before they hit everyone
  • The device registry made per-device logout straightforward to reason about
  • The runbook from the load-test drill was actually used during the spike

What went wrong

  • Refresh-token rotation raced under concurrent tab bursts
  • The status page lagged real incidents by up to an hour
  • Signup emails hit a soft bounce-rate limit with one mailbox provider

Learnings

Load-testing against the real token shape catches what synthetic tests miss. The drill used realistic payloads and device counts, so the canary cohort stayed ahead of the failure curve during the spike.

Rotation must be idempotent per token family before GA. The race only appeared under concurrent tabs, which means the shared-write assumption held until real users arrived.

Action items

  • Make refresh-token rotation idempotent per token family
  • Add a status-page integration that mirrors real incident state
  • Backfill email deliverability monitoring and bounce-rate alerts
  • Publish the GA runbook with the load and incident lessons baked in
